Method

One question of every piece of data: if this left the building, what would it cost?

The answers sort everything into three tiers, and each tier gets the AI it deserves. Open data goes to the best cloud model on day one. Internal data runs in a private deployment you control, with access by role and a log of everything. The crown jewels live on local hardware with an open-weight model and no cable to the outside.

Between the second and third tier is a wall with a single gate. Every request that touches the crown jewels, whether a person, a file or an AI, presents a one-time ticket bound to one purpose and one time window. The gatekeeper says yes or no, spends the ticket, and writes it to a ledger no one can edit.

Research programme

The quantum gate: a ticket no computer can forge.

Our research line asks one question: how do you make the gate's ticket hold for twenty years, through the arrival of quantum computers? Today's answer is built from parts you can buy. Tomorrow's is in the laboratory.

Today · deployable

Ticket keys from a quantum random number generator, so no one can predict them. Signatures with ML-DSA, the NIST post-quantum standard of 2024, so no known computer, quantum ones included, can forge them.

Next · in development

Full post-quantum encryption of the crown-jewel store itself, against "harvest now, decrypt later": the adversary who copies your files today and waits for the machine that opens them.

Horizon · research

True quantum tokens: physical tickets that cannot be copied at all. Demonstrated over a few kilometres of fibre in 2024. A research project, not a product, and we say so.

We never say "unbreakable". The honest claim is "no known attack", and we write the date next to it. [CONFIRM: any statement about the founders' own quantum-technology experience goes here, only if it is literally true]
